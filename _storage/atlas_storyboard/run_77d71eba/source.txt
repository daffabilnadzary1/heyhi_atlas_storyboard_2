PowerOne Tiles
- PowerOne tiles include Salesforce, Epicor Mobile, AI Desktop, Pico Concierge, Partners, and Helpdesk for quick access.
- Clicking a tile like Helpdesk takes you directly into the related internal system or service portal.

Human Resources Content
- Human Resources content on the intranet centralizes HR forms, guidance and links instead of scattered emails.
- Using these pages ensures you follow the same documented procedures as the rest of the company.

Comparing Related Pages
- Comparing related pages, such as Expense Claim, Per Diem rates and Travel Requisition, helps you understand complete workflows.
- This approach lets you build clear checklists of required receipts, approvals, and applicable rates.
