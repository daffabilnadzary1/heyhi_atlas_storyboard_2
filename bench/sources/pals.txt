Annual Leave Basics
Employees receive 30 calendar / running days of annual leave per year, including Fridays, Saturdays.
Calendar or running days means weekends count towards leave, not only working days counted.

Leave Earning Period
Annual leave earning period runs from 1 Nov to 31 Oct every year consistently.
Planning leave within this earning period helps match taken days with accrued entitlements.

Probationary Leave Access
You can apply for Annual Leave after completing probation successfully in the company.
Before probation ends, the system access for annual leave is not normally available for employees.

Emergency Leave During Probation
If there is an emergency before probation, employees should discuss with immediate manager directly.
This emergency discussion may allow flexibility while still respecting probationary employment conditions.

Carry Forward Leave
Annual leave can be carry forward up to 3 months with prior approval.
This carry forward flexibility still depends on permission from the Head, Middle East.
