1. INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE
Welcome to TechNova Solutions. This Human Resources Policy Manual is designed to provide employees with a clear understanding of our company's expectations, workplace standards, and the benefits available to our team members. The policies outlined herein apply to all regular, full-time, part-time, and temporary employees unless otherwise specified. Management reserves the right to modify, revoke, suspend, or terminate any policy at its sole discretion, with or without prior notice.

2. EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY (EEO)
TechNova Solutions is fundamentally committed to the principle of equal employment opportunity. We prohibit discrimination and harassment of any kind based on race, color, sex, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, genetic information, pregnancy, or any other protected characteristic as outlined by federal, state, or local laws. This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including recruiting, hiring, placement, promotion, termination, layoff, recall, transfer, leaves of absence, compensation, and training.

3. ANTI-HARASSMENT AND BULLYING POLICY
We strive to maintain a work environment that fosters mutual employee respect and promotes congenial relationships between employees. We explicitly prohibit any form of harassment, joking remarks, or other abusive conduct (including verbal, non-verbal, and physical conduct) directed at an individual's race, sexual orientation, disability, or religion.
3.1 Reporting: Employees who believe they have been subjected to harassment must immediately report the incident to their direct supervisor or the HR department.
3.2 Non-Retaliation: TechNova Solutions strictly forbids retaliation against anyone who, in good faith, reports a violation of this policy or participates in an investigation.
